## Important limitation

This is a preliminary collision screening, not a legal trademark clearance. Search-engine indexing is incomplete, and trademark risk includes visually, phonetically and conceptually similar marks. Before commercial release, search DPMAregister, EUIPO/TMview and WIPO for at least:

- `DocuWunder`
- `DokuWunder`
- `Doc Wunder`
- `WunderDoc`
- Similar `Docu*` and `*Wunder` marks

Relevant Nice classes are likely to include class 9 (downloadable software/mobile apps) and class 42 (software services/SaaS), with the final scope depending on the business model.
